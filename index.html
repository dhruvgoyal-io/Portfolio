Quantitative Research & Projects

Algorithmic Numerical Solvers & Matrix Decompositions

Constructed automated computational workflows to solve large-scale systems of linear equations, comparing numerical stability, error propagation, and time complexity across LU Decomposition, QR Factorization via Gram-Schmidt/Householder reflections, and Singular Value Decomposition (SVD).

MATLAB Scientific Computing Numerical Linear Algebra

Stochastic Asset Price Dynamics & Volatility Modeling

Simulated continuous-time financial stochastic processes utilizing Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM) and jump-diffusion models. Analyzed stationary vs. non-stationary properties in market time series, implementing statistical tests (ADF, KPSS) and evaluating historical volatility regimes.

Python Pandas & NumPy Stochastic Calculus Monte Carlo

Theoretical Optimization Dynamics in Deep Networks

Analyzing the geometry of high-dimensional non-convex loss surfaces. Investigating how gradient descent trajectories, momentum terms, and learning rate schedules escape saddle points and achieve generalization in deep neural networks.
